Shortcuts & Instructions

This page is being read aloud automatically. You do not need to look at the screen - just listen. Tap anywhere to pause or resume listening controls at the bottom.

Talking to BlindMate

  • Say "Hey BlindMate" (or just "BlindMate") anytime, followed by what you want - BlindMate is always listening for this.
  • Double-tap anywhere on the screen to start giving a voice command immediately, without saying the wake word first.

Touch Gestures (work anywhere on screen)

  • Long-press and hold for about one second to turn object detection on or off.
  • Double-tap to start a voice command.
  • Triple-tap quickly to instantly read aloud any text in front of the camera - great for medicine labels and documents.

Navigation

  • Tap the Navigation button for the fastest way to start walking directions - no wake word needed. It stops object detection, asks "Where would you like to go?", listens for your answer, then starts guiding you turn by turn. Object detection automatically turns back on for the walk, so you'll still be warned about people, vehicles, and other obstacles along the way.
  • Or say "Hey BlindMate, take me to" followed by any place - same result, using your voice the whole way through.

Memory

  • Tap the Memory button to hear a summary of what's been remembered recently - what objects, and where they were last seen.
  • Say "Where is my" followed by an object name, like "where is my phone", to hear about that one specific thing.
  • Say "Remember this place as" followed by a name, like "remember this place as bathroom", to save your current location.

Describe Scene

  • Tap the Describe Scene button, or say "Hey BlindMate, describe the scene", to hear a spoken description of what's in front of the camera right now.

Emergency

  • Shake your phone firmly to alert your emergency contacts with your location. You get about six seconds to cancel by touching the screen, in case it was an accident.
  • Press the red SOS button, or Ctrl + Shift + E on a keyboard, to send the alert immediately.
  • After an alert, your contacts also get a live location link that updates for two hours. Say "I am safe" to stop sharing.
  • Say "Hey BlindMate, emergency" or "I need help now" to do the same thing by voice.

Keyboard Shortcuts (laptop or Bluetooth keyboard)

  • Ctrl + V - start a voice command.
  • Ctrl + S - turn object detection on or off.
  • Ctrl + G - start navigation: it asks where you want to go.
  • Ctrl + L - turn on location (GPS).
  • Ctrl + Shift + O - read text in front of the camera.
  • Ctrl + Shift + E - send the emergency alert now.
  • Ctrl + Shift + C - find out which rupee note or coin is in front of the camera.
  • Ctrl + Shift + B - hear your phone battery level.

All Voice Commands

  • "Start detection" and "Stop detection".
  • "Take me to" a place, "Preview route to" a place, "Stop navigation", "Emergency stop", "Show map".
  • "Where am I" or "Enable location".
  • "Read this" or "What does this say".
  • "Describe the scene" or any question about what is around you.
  • "Where is my phone" - last seen location of an object.
  • "Remember this place as" a name, then "Where is" that name.
  • "Change language to Hindi" (or Kannada, Tamil, Telugu).
  • "Change tone to calm" (friendly, formal, energetic, calm, robotic).
  • "Emergency", "SOS", "I need help now", or "I'm in danger". In Hindi say "bachao" or "emergency"; in Kannada, Tamil and Telugu the words for "save me" or "emergency" also work.
  • "How much battery do I have?" or "Battery status" - tells you the battery level and whether it is charging.
  • "I am safe" or "Stop sharing location" - ends live location sharing after an emergency.
  • "What note is this?", "Identify the money" or "How much money is this?" - tells you the rupee note or coin in front of the camera.
  • "Show shortcuts" or "Help" - opens this page.

Useful things to say

  • "Take me to the library" - start walking directions to any place.
  • "Where is my phone" - hear where an object was last seen.
  • "Remember this place as bathroom" - save your current location with a name.
  • "Where is the bathroom" - get directions back to a saved place.
  • "Read this" or "what does this say" - read text aloud.
  • "Describe the scene" - hear what's in front of the camera.
  • "What did I say" or "read my history" - hear your recent activity.
  • Ask any general question, like "what's the capital of France".

On a computer keyboard

  • Ctrl + S - turn object detection on or off.
  • Ctrl + V - start a voice command.
  • Ctrl + L - turn on GPS / location.
  • Ctrl + G - navigation shortcut (same as tapping the Navigation button).
  • Ctrl + Shift + O - read text aloud (same as triple-tap).
  • Ctrl + Shift + E - emergency SOS (same as shaking the phone).

Opening the app without touching your phone screen

  • Voice assistant: say "Hey Google, open BlindMate" on Android, or "Hey Siri, open BlindMate" on iPhone - this works once the app has been installed to your home screen.
  • Back Tap: a phone setting that opens the app when you tap the back of your phone twice or three times. On Android, this is under Settings, System, Gestures, Quick Tap. On iPhone, it's under Settings, Accessibility, Touch, Back Tap. A sighted helper needs to set this up once.
  • NFC tag: a small sticker, on a keychain or cane for example, that opens the app the moment your phone touches it. A sighted helper can set this up once using a free NFC writing app.

Other buttons on the main screen

  • Home, History, and Settings are at the bottom of the screen for a sighted helper to use if needed.
  • Everything on this page can also be done by voice - you never need to find a specific button.
